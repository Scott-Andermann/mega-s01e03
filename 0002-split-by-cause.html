0002 · Duplicates share a cause, not a subject

owner Scott-Andermann proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Deciders: Scott-Andermann, support desk · Replaces: 0001 · Duplicates share a subject line

Context

Under 0001 the desk merged reports on subject line alone and nobody read the body. Since April that put every date report into #4471, marked known, with one fix queued. Its reports hold three problems with three different fixes. Engineering’s 5 October update fixed one and retired the form behind another. The third is still open, and #4471 hides it.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they share a cause, not a subject line. To split a big ticket, the desk:

  1. counts unique report ids, because the export pages overlap and repeat rows;
  2. places each report by its tenant: does the tenant’s locale write the day first, and which booking form do they run (causes.ts, locales.ts);
  3. reads the body, and moves a report whose body shows a different failure (the desk’s desk-notes.json) to that failure;
  4. ignores the intake tag. It is a guess from the reporter’s browser.

  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY but reads the tenant’s day-first order. Won’t fix, booking-v3 retired; #2 closed.

#1 gives the count the desk’s own table on #4471 gives: it files every report by tenant, so its parser count also holds the one-day moves.

Consequences

Merging on subject is fast, but it closed a bug that nobody fixed. A cause costs one lookup and one read per report. That is cheaper than a known ticket hiding an open bug.
