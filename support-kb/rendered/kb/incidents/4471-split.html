parser: day-first dates read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so any date with both parts at 12 or under comes back swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. App, email and booking-v2 form reports. This is the bug #4471 describes. Fixed on 5 October.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the input the tenant’s way, day first. People do what the hint says and get a swapped date. Only form submissions on booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch this. Engineering retired booking-v3 instead of fixing it, so no new reports should come from it.

one-day: dates move by one day

These are not swaps. The booking lands one day early, and the same meeting shows different days in different time zones (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). That is time-zone handling, not field order. The desk noted these reports but still counted them under the parser, so they look closed with #4471. They are not: the parser fix reads field order, not time zones. Still open.
